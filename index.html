Tips and shortcuts

Palette

  • Click a swatch to select it. Shift-click selects a range, Ctrl-click (⌘ on Mac) adds or removes one.
  • Drag a swatch to move it. Swap or Insert is set under the palette.
  • Right-click a swatch, or press Delete, to clear it.
  • Arrow keys move between swatches.
  • Lock keeps colors safe from presets, sorting, Adjust, fills and opening a file.

Color lab

  • The top bar is the saved swatch, the bottom bar is the color you're trying. Nothing is saved until Replace swatch (or Enter in the hex or number boxes).
  • Click a Degrees, Tones or Shades chip to try it. Drag any chip onto a swatch to save it there.
  • Harmonies: click a color to add it to the first empty slot. Drag the wheel's dots to change the angle.
  • Click a section title to fold it away.

Pins

  • Pin holds the lab color aside (or click an empty pin).
  • Click a pin to load it into the lab. Drag pins to swap them, or onto a swatch.
  • Drag a swatch into a pin. Right-click a pin to remove it.
  • Pins stay in this browser between visits.

Image

  • Drop, paste (Ctrl+V) or choose an image.
  • Click it to pick a color into the lab. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Fill palette from image makes a whole palette: pick a mood and how many colors.

Files and keys

  • Drop a .swatches file anywhere on the page to open it.
  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redo.
  • Export card saves a picture of the palette with its hex codes.
