# Palette Lab

A free browser tool for making and editing Procreate `.swatches` palettes.

**Use it here:** https://camchromatic.com/palette-lab.html

Your files stay on your computer. Images and palettes are never uploaded anywhere, and no AI runs in this tool.

## What it does

- Build a palette of up to 30 colors, or start from a preset (Grays, Basic, Random).
- Open an existing `.swatches` file, edit it, and export it back for Procreate.
- Pick colors from your own image, or fill the whole palette from it.
- Try shades, tones and color harmonies, sort colors, lock the ones you want to keep, and blend between them.
- Copy or paste palettes as hex codes, and save a PNG card of the palette.

## How to use it

1. Open the link above, or download `index.html` and open it in your browser.
2. Make your palette, then press **Export .swatches**.
3. Send the file to your iPad and open it in Procreate. It appears in your Palettes.

Press the **?** button in the tool for tips and shortcuts.

The page needs an internet connection to load one small library (JSZip, for making `.swatches` files).
